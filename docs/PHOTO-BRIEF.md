# Photo brief, what to supply, and why each one earns its slot

Two slots remain. Deliberately two and not five: badminton frames this site
but engineering fills it, and a visitor counts photographs rather than
sections, so a wall of sport imagery would undo that balance.

No stock photography is used on this site. Generic shots of anonymous players
on an elite player's portfolio read as filler, and filler next to a real
credential makes the real credential look decorative. Every slot below is empty
until a real photograph fills it, and the layout is built to look deliberate
while they are empty.

Drop files in `src/assets/` using the filenames given. Each slot degrades to a
labelled plate, so nothing breaks before they arrive.

---

## 1. Podium or medal presentation, **highest value**

`badminton_podium.jpg` · landscape · ≥2000px wide

**Why:** this is the only photograph that makes *National Elite Champion*
verifiable at a glance. Everything else on the site asserts the credential in
text; this proves it. A recruiter who scrolls past the words will still stop at
a podium.

**What works:** you on the podium with the medal, ideally with a Badminton
Canada or tournament backdrop in frame. Branding and signage in shot are a
feature, not clutter, they are the evidence.

**What doesn't:** a cropped close-up of a medal alone. No context, no proof.

---

## 2. Mid-rally action, **the memorability slot**

`badminton_action.jpg` · landscape · ≥2400px wide · fast shutter

**Why:** this is what makes the site remembered rather than read. A jump smash
at full extension communicates athleticism in a way no sentence does, and it is
the one image that justifies badminton framing the whole page rather than
sitting in a footnote.

**What works:** airborne, racket back or at contact, shuttle visible in frame if
possible. Shot from the side or low, not from behind the baseline. Arena
lighting is fine, the site ground is near-black, so dark backgrounds composite
well.

**What doesn't:** posed shots holding a racket. They read as a stock photo even
when they are not.

## 3. Headshot - SUPPLIED

`src/assets/headshot.jpg` · 448x448 · in place since 2026-10-11

Done. It sits at the foot of the hero, beside the name.

Note it is the most formal image on the site: studio lighting, blazer, navy
backdrop. That suits fintech and reads as serious; it is slightly buttoned-up
for an early-stage startup. Worth keeping in mind if you ever shoot another.

## Rights and attribution

Supply only photographs you own or have permission to publish. Tournament
photography is frequently owned by the federation or a contracted photographer
even when the subject is you, if a shot came from an event, confirm you may
use it before it ships. Where a photographer must be credited, add the credit
to the asset's entry and it will render in the caption rather than being
dropped.
